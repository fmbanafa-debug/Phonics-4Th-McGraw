Phonics Rule & Tip

Words with the same short vowel sound can be spelled in different ways.

  • The words ink, build, and gym each have a short i sound.
  • The words egg and head each have a short e sound.
  • Spelling Tip: Short a words are usually spelled using a (flap, tap, clap). Short o words are usually spelled using o (lot, stop, plod).
